Fullstack tech lead with 25 years of experience, including 11 years in retail banking at BPCE. There I led the design and rollout of Mysys, the portal advisers use to launch all their banking processes: first for the 50,000 Caisses d'Épargne advisers, then for around fifteen Banques Populaires and 30,000 more users.

I work closely with the business: workshops with the business team (MOA) and product owners, writing and reviewing specifications, demos, direct exchanges with users, and acting on field feedback. What matters to me is that the tool saves time for the people who use it every day.

On the technical side, I specialise in TypeScript, front end and back end (Angular, Node.js / NestJS), with a solid Java / Spring background that often proves useful where legacy meets modern. I lead teams of 4 to 10 developers with code quality as a top priority: code reviews, peer programming, gitflow, CI/CD, strict linting and Sonar coverage. Since 2026, I have been rolling out Claude Code across Allianz's development teams.
