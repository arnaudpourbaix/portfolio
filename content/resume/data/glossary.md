# Translation glossary (French → English)

## Keep as written

- Company and product names: BPCE, Banques Populaires, Caisses d'Épargne, AG2R La Mondiale, Norsys, Norauto, Unis, ELOSI, Kiloutou, Pictime Group, Astek, Auchan, Proxiad, Allianz, Allianz France, AID Micro, Phildar, Grain de Malice, Myway, Mysys, CefSharp, Dynatrace, PNData, Oui Energy, Winshop.
- Technology names, exactly as spelled in the French file (Java EE, PL/SQL, NestJS, Angular (9-17), …).
- "Tech lead", "peer programming", "from scratch", "monorepo(s)", "gitflow", "pull requests".
- Place names (Villeneuve d'Ascq, Roubaix, Lille, Paris, Lens).

## Style

- Plain, professional British/US-neutral English; keep bullets short and start them with a verb or noun as in French.
- Keep French typographic spacing out of English (no space before `:`).
